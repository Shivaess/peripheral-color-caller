Peripheral color caller

Keep your eyes on a center target. When a color is called, find it with your peripheral vision.

This browser has no voice available, so calls will be silent. Try Chrome or Safari, check that the phone isn't on silent, or turn on "Also show the call on screen" and have a partner read the calls aloud.

How to set it up
  1. Put four cards on a wall in a rectangle, one in each corner, each with a dot: blue, pink, orange, green. Put a small target in the middle.
  2. Tap Shuffle card positions and arrange your cards to match, so you don't memorize where each color is.
  3. Stand where your therapist recommends, with your eyes on the center target.
  4. Set the timing your therapist suggests, tap Test voice to check sound, then tap Start and set the phone down.
  5. When a color is called, point to or touch it without moving your eyes off the center.
Ready

Shuffle, then move your cards on the wall to match.

Turn your volume up. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or your symptoms climb, stop and rest.
