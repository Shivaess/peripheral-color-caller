# Peripheral color caller

**Live site:** _(link added once GitHub Pages is up)_

A one-page web tool for peripheral vision chart practice. It speaks the colors
**blue, pink, orange, and green** at random, varied intervals. You keep your eyes
on a center target and find each called color using your peripheral vision.

## Setting up the wall

1. Make four cards, each with one colored dot: blue, pink, orange, green.
2. Put them on a wall in a rectangle, one card in each corner.
3. Put a small target (a dot or sticker) in the middle of the rectangle.
4. In the tool, tap **Shuffle card positions** and move your cards to match, so
   you don't memorize where each color is.
5. Stand where your therapist recommends, set the timing, tap **Test voice**,
   then **Start**, and keep your eyes on the center target.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. There is no account, no server, and no data
collected. Your settings are saved only on your own device (browser local
storage).

## Voice

The calls use your device's built-in speech (the Web Speech API), so the voice
depends on your phone or computer. **Chrome, Safari, and Edge work best.** Turn
your volume up and check the phone isn't on silent. If no voice is available,
the page shows a warning; you can turn on "Also show the call on screen" and
have a partner read the calls aloud.
